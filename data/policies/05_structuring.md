# Structuring (FIN-POL-041)

Cash transactions over $10,000 require a Currency Transaction Report (CTR). Structuring is breaking
cash into smaller amounts to avoid that report, and it is a federal offense regardless of the source
of funds.

**Rule ST-1:** 3 or more cash deposits between $8,000 and $9,999.99 within 7 days indicates
structuring. Disposition: **file_sar**.

Business accounts with regular cash revenue that includes deposits above $10,000 (for which CTRs
are filed) and varied amounts below the threshold are consistent with normal operations; **close**
unless Rule ST-1 is met.
